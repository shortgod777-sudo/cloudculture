FAQ

Good questions

How does mail delivery work?

Orders ship Monday to Friday within 24 hours in a plain, odour-proof parcel with no branding. You get a tracking number, and delivery takes 2–5 business days in most of Canada. An adult must sign and show ID on delivery.

What's the minimum order and shipping cost?

The minimum is $100 per order. Mail shipping is $10 and local courier is $5, both free on orders over $150. Pickup is coming soon.

How do subscriptions work?

Choose weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or every 2 months in your bag. You save 10% on every shipment, and we email a reminder before each parcel ships. Skip, pause or cancel anytime. The $100 minimum applies to each shipment.

How do I pay?

The checkout is a demo. No payment instructions are sent and no payment is accepted.

How can I contact Cloud Culture?

For general questions, email support@cloudculture.ca. For checkout or order questions, email orders@cloudculture.ca.

What ID do I need?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID showing you are of legal age: 19 in most provinces and 21 in Québec. The person on the order must be the one who signs for it.

Is the product lab tested?

Product cards illustrate the catalog layout. The prototype does not verify product testing or licensing.

What if I'm new to edibles?

Start low and go slow. Try 2.5–5mg, wait at least two hours, and don't redose early.
